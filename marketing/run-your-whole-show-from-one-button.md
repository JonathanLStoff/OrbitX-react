# Run Your Whole Show From One Button

Whether it's a school play, a talent show, a band night, or a livestream, OrbitX puts your whole show in one place. Music, sound effects, background videos, and pictures all sit in one playlist, and you fire them on cue.

## The tour

1. **One playlist, one button.** Songs, sound effects, videos, and pictures live in one cue list. Hit the big green GO button or the spacebar, and the next cue starts right on time.
2. **Cut out the dead air.** Open a song's Edit tab, type new start and end points, and see the part you keep. Trim in seconds, no fiddling.
3. **Loop background music smoothly.** Turn on Loop, and a video follows the cue before it. Background tracks keep going with no awkward gap.
4. **Let cues count down themselves.** Set a countdown in the Timing tab, and the cue fires by itself. Sound effects and scene changes happen on time while you focus on the performers.
5. **Slide the volume down.** In the Mix tab, lower a cue's volume so the speech cuts through.
6. **Add effects in seconds.** The Plugins tab adds effects to any cue. A touch of echo can make a voice fill the whole hall.

## Works where you work

Build your show on a laptop and open the same .obx project file on Windows, Mac, or Linux. It also runs on iPhone, iPad, and Android, so you can make changes backstage.

## Built for show night

Every edit saves automatically in the background, so a crash won't wipe out your work. If a speaker or USB cable gets bumped, OrbitX reconnects on its own.
